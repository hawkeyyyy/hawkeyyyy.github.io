/* Summary */

                

  Final-year Computer Engineering student working at the intersection of GIS, remote sensing,
  and machine learning. I build pipelines that go from raw GeoTIFFs to decision-ready outputs:
  reprojection/CRS alignment, feature rasters (distance/elevation/hydrology), tiling, training, and post-processing.




  My current work focuses on geospatial mapping problems like flood susceptibility and landfill suitability, using
  CNN-based models and segmentation approaches. I care about clean experiments, readable notebooks, and deployable utilities.
